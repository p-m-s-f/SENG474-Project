# SENG474-Project

This repo is home to our SENG 474 term project. Its authors are Parris Mook-Sang-Forbes, Max Patchell, and Varun Manon.

Our goal is classifying aerial wildfire images into three operational risk tiers: low, medium, and high. False positive detections are minimized by raining a model to differentiation geniune fire development from confounding environmental variables (e.g., fog, weather, terrain, foliage). This ensures critical firefighting resources are deployed based on actual threats rather than algorithmic errors.
